Audio engineer focused on the perceptual evaluation of machine-learning-based audio.

My MSc research at the University of York investigated how listeners perceive neural models of analogue audio gear, from high-gain guitar amplifiers to EQs and compressors, using listening experiments and statistical analysis. My broader interests span generative audio, neural amp modelling, spatial and immersive audio, psychoacoustics, and music post-production with emerging technologies.

WANKA

A digital pututu (shell trumpet) you play with hand gestures via webcam inside a virtual reconstruction of the Doble Ménsula gallery at Chavín de Huántar, Peru. The gallery reverb is a head-tracked B-format convolution decoded to binaural, and the modelled room is analysed according to ISO 3382‑1.

SoundSpells

A VR game for PICO 4 where sound becomes something you act on. You cast chorus, echo, pitch-shift and distortion "spells" with mid-air wand gestures to free four statues that whisper riddles. Audio is spatialised binaurally with Steam Audio, and telemetry from 20 test sessions was analysed to measure gesture-recognition rates.

Binaural radio drama & virtual pitch explorer

A 30-second binaural scene: a door opens behind you, a man walks in a circle around you as he talks, a radio switches on to your left, and a voice flies over your head. Sources are rendered with SADIE II binaural impulse responses, both static and moving in azimuth and elevation, using overlap-add convolution with smoothing between responses. Effects are applied before spatialisation.

🎧 Use headphones

Also included: an interactive virtual-pitch tool with six missing-fundamental test cases (harmonic, F2-dominant, high-frequency and inharmonic tones, plus a melody with randomised spectra).
